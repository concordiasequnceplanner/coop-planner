2026 Montréal RAMS

Reliability · Asset Management · Safety — a full day of technical exchange, industry–academia dialogue, student participation and networking

Event recap

A great day for Montréal’s RAMS community

Thank you to every speaker, panelist, student team and attendee who brought experience, questions and energy to the day. The program connected reliability, asset management and safety across transportation, aerospace, energy, AI, data, medical applications and field practice.

Looking ahead

Reliability Centre initiative at the Gina Cody School

The event also included the announcement of work toward creating a Reliability Centre at Concordia University’s Gina Cody School of Engineering and Computer Science, with the objective of strengthening structured reliability education and industry–academia collaboration. More details will be shared as the initiative develops.
