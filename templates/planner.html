📋 How to Use This Tool
🖱️ ➜ 📦

DRAG and DROP courses into terms

Click and hold a course box, then drag it to the desired term

Standard sequence:
click CEGEP (yellow) → Standard sequence (green) → place TEs → select reason 1 → submit

Change of Sequence:

  • Your completed courses are already loaded in the system - you'll find them pre-populated.
    • CEGEP students: Click CEGEP ECP→Y0 (yellow button on the right) to place all ECP courses.
  • If you're repeating a course, add a new course using "Repeat a course" menu (right).
  • Place your Work Terms (WTs) and place the remaining courses:
    • Fast Track: Set the max number of courses per remaining terms and use the AUTO PLACE feature (blue button on the right) to automatically position remaining courses. The tool autoplace logic has limitations - double check, it's your responsibility.
    • Manually: Place the courses by dragging them to the desired terms.
  • WARNINGS are for your information (FYI) - they alert you to potential concerns but don't block submission.
  • ERRORS should be addressed before submission - these indicate issues that need correction (see also #8).
  • Select the appropriate "Sequence Submission Reason"
  • Click SUBMIT even if you're not completely sure - your MIAE CO-OP AD will review and provide feedback or approval.
  • If you encounter errors you cannot resolve or the tool is not working/gives error messages, take a screenshot and email it to coop_miae@concordia.ca

📋 MIAE CO-OP Rules

📌 GPA Requirement

1. ✅ GPA_24CR ≥ 2.7 → Eligible for a Work Term

2. ❌ GPA_24CR < 2.7 → Not eligible; sequence adjusted to prioritize academics

** If GPA_24CR < 2.7 → stay in CO-OP but no WT until GPA_24CR ≥ 2.7

** GPA_24CR = moving GPA computed over your most recent 24 CR.

3. CGPA ≥ 2.5 to remain in CO-OP (below → withdrawn from CO-OP, no impact on MIAE Program)

📌 Work Term Rules

1. WT1 prerequisites: ≥30 program CR AND > 2 terms with the Institute

2. ENCS 282 required: must be completed before WT1

3. Max 2 Summer WTs

4. No 3 consecutive WTs

5. Max 1 course during a WT: requires written employer permission

6. Before WT3, Full-time Fall/Winter required: ≥12 CR (Summer exempt)

7. No WT during Capstone: 490A/490B

8. Cannot graduate on a WT: at least 1 CORE or PRG course after WT3

⚠ Sequence Deviations

Any deviation (including one or more courses not taken as scheduled) requires a sequence change. You must re-submit your updated sequence for approval in the planner tool.
