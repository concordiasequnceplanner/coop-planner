Service and price

Annual membership and student sponsorship

Membership provides access to the chapter activities and benefits described on the website, subject to any activity-specific conditions.

General membership

Professional Member

Student membership

Full-Time Student Member

$10 CAD / year

Available to eligible full-time students at any level of study, subject to available sponsored places.

Separate sponsorship contribution

Sponsor a Student

$30 CAD / student place

Each separate CAD $30 sponsorship contribution creates one student membership place. Sponsorship alone does not create or renew the sponsor’s membership.

Membership and Student Sponsorship are separate payments and may be receipted separately. Unused sponsored student places carry forward until used. SRE Montréal may request reasonable evidence of current full-time student status. No GST/QST is currently added to the stated membership fees. SRE Montréal does not add a fee for Interac e-Transfer; your financial institution may charge its own fees.

Term

Membership year, renewal and year-end payments

  • Membership is annual and there is no automatic renewal.
  • A membership payment received and accepted on or before December 25 is applied to the current calendar year if that member does not already have that membership year covered.
  • If the current calendar year is already covered, the payment is applied to the next membership year unless SRE Montréal agrees to an administrative correction or other arrangement.
  • A membership payment received and accepted from December 26 through December 31 is applied to the following membership year.
  • Student membership remains subject to the availability of a sponsored student membership place.
Payment

Interac e-Transfer

Payment is made in Canadian dollars by Interac e-Transfer to sremontreal@gmail.com. Before paying, the member must review the membership category, applicable amount and information being submitted.

Payment constitutes a request for membership under these terms. SRE Montréal confirms acceptance by email and states the category, amount paid and membership term. A copy of or link to these terms is provided with the confirmation.

Cancellation and refunds

Policy

Membership fees are non-refundable after membership is activated, except for duplicate payments, payments made in error, SRE Montréal’s inability to provide an accepted membership, or where a refund is required by law. Requests must be sent to sremontreal@gmail.com.

Personal information

Privacy

Membership information is handled under the Privacy Policy. SRE Montréal requests only information required to administer the membership record and meet applicable obligations.

Language

French and English versions

The French version is provided before the membership contract is concluded. The English version may govern only after the member has received the French version and expressly chosen to contract in English.
