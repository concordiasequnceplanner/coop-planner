Membership & student support

Join the community — and help open the door for the next generation

Professional membership and sponsoring a student are separate payments. Members can sponsor one or more full-time students enrolled in Canadian programs, while eligible students access membership at a reduced rate when sponsored places are available.

Professional membership

Professional Member

$50 CAD / year

Annual SRE Montréal membership with access to chapter member benefits.

Support the next generation

Sponsor a Student

$30 CAD / student place

Each separate CAD $30 sponsorship creates one membership place for a full-time student enrolled in a Canadian program. You may sponsor more than one student.

Public members have each sponsored student place recognized with a handshake icon beside their name in the Members directory.

Student membership

Full-Time Student Member

$10 CAD / year

Available to eligible full-time students at any level of study, subject to the number of sponsored places available.
