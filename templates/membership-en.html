Sponsor a Student

$30 CAD / student place

A separate sponsorship contribution available to SRE Montréal members. Each CAD $30 creates one membership place for a full-time student enrolled in a Canadian program, who pays only CAD $10 for the membership

You may sponsor more than one student by contributing CAD $30 for each student membership place.

Unused sponsored places carry forward until used. For members who choose to be publicly listed, each sponsored student place is recognized with a handshake icon beside the member’s name in the public Members directory.

If you would like to sponsor a specific student, provide the student’s name with the sponsorship payment or contact SRE Montréal

Payments

Before a membership payment: first review the French Membership Terms. The English Membership Terms and the Privacy Policy are also available. Review the applicable amount and your information before sending the transfer.

Québec language requirement: the French Membership Terms are made available before you choose an English-language contract. If, after reviewing the French version, you expressly wish to be bound by the English version, state “I wish to contract in English” in your membership email.

Interac e-Transfer

Send payments to sremontreal@gmail.com

Membership and sponsorship are separate payments. If you are paying both, send separate Interac e-Transfers so each transaction receives its own receipt.

In the Interac message/comment, identify the member or sponsor with:

  • Full name — LAST, FIRST, MI
  • Company / organization affiliation
  • Preferred address
  • City and province/state
  • Postal / ZIP code
  • Preferred phone number
  • Email address
  • Payment purpose: Professional Membership, Full-Time Student Membership, Sponsor a Student, or SRE Sponsorship (describe the purpose)
  • For Sponsor a Student payments: number of student membership places sponsored and, if applicable, the name of a specific student you wish to support
  • Listed name, if different from the full name
  • Public LinkedIn profile URL, if you want it displayed in the public member directory
  • Chapter member-list preference: Public (listed) or Private (not listed)*

After acceptance of a membership payment, SRE Montréal sends a written confirmation by email stating the amount paid and membership term. Each separate payment receives its own payment receipt.

A payment receipt is not a charitable tax receipt**

* Member-list preference can be changed at any time; updates may be subject to normal processing delays

** Receipt issuance may be subject to normal processing delays
