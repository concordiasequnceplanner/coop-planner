Membership payment

Before payment: first review the French Membership Terms. The English Membership Terms and the Privacy Policy are also available. Review the membership fee and your information before sending the transfer. Do not pay if you do not accept the applicable terms.

Québec language requirement: the French Membership Terms are made available before you choose an English-language contract. If, after reviewing the French version, you expressly wish to be bound by the English version, state “I wish to contract in English” in your membership email.

After acceptance, SRE Montréal sends a written membership confirmation by email stating the amount paid and membership term, with a link to the applicable terms.

Interac e-Transfer

Send the membership payment to sremontreal@gmail.com

In the Interac message/comment, identify the member with:

  • Full name — LAST, FIRST, MI
  • Company / organization affiliation
  • Preferred address
  • City and province/state
  • Postal / ZIP code
  • Preferred phone number
  • Email address
  • Chapter member-list preference: Public (listed) or Private (not listed)*

SRE Montréal sends a payment receipt by email after the transfer is received. A payment receipt is not a charitable tax receipt**

* Member-list preference can be changed at any time; updates may be subject to normal processing delays

** Receipt issuance may be subject to normal processing delays
