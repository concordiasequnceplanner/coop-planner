Sponsorships, donations & contributions

Interac e-Transfer

Send contributions to sremontreal@gmail.com

Information related to contributions is handled under our privacy policy.

In the Interac message/comment, include:

  • Legal name of the individual or organization
  • If paid on behalf of an organization, full name and role/title of the person initiating the transfer
  • Purpose or designation of funds: Sponsorship, Donation, Support Contribution, Event/Invoice Reference, or Other
  • Any reference or identifying details needed to match the payment

SRE Montréal sends a payment receipt by email after the transfer is received. A payment receipt is not a charitable tax receipt**

** Receipt issuance may be subject to normal processing delays

Membership payment

Before payment: review the Membership Terms and the Privacy Policy. Review the membership category, amount and your information before sending the transfer. Do not pay if you do not accept the terms.

After acceptance, SRE Montréal sends a written membership confirmation by email stating the category, amount paid and membership term, with a link to the applicable terms.

Québec language rule: the French version of the Membership Terms is provided first. If, after receiving it, you expressly wish to contract in English, state “I wish to contract in English” in your membership email.

Interac e-Transfer

Send the membership payment to sremontreal@gmail.com

In the Interac message/comment, identify the member with:

  • Full name — LAST, FIRST, MI
  • Email address
  • Membership category
  • Chapter member-list preference: Public (listed) or Private (not listed)*
  • Affiliation, postal address and phone: only when required to complete or maintain the membership record

SRE Montréal sends a payment receipt by email after the transfer is received. A payment receipt is not a charitable tax receipt**

* Member-list preference can be changed at any time; updates may be subject to normal processing delays

** Receipt issuance may be subject to normal processing delays
